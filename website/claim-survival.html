01 / RIVER COMMUNITY

Survives Conditional

Initial claim

Detected fish analysis-label richness broadens downstream in the balanced River Conwy series.

→

Credibility audit

A 72-family multiverse varied panel, unit, taxonomy, threshold, effort handling and distance; it also retained every declared one-library draw and reference leave-out.

→

Surviving wording

The downstream descriptive direction is highly stable across valid specifications, but the preregistered full-multiverse robustness label is withheld; the restricted reference PERMANOVA remains p = 2/28 = 0.0714.

Inspect gate, limitation and evidence
Gate result
Full robustness gate failed: 64/72 families were valid (88.9%, below 90%). Direction stayed positive in 64/64 valid families and 3,600/3,600 draws; 0/75 reference leave-outs reversed it.
Limitation
Eight rCLR families were undefined because some real 154-unit communities were empty. Detected-label richness is not true richness, and the analysis does not establish a section centroid shift or causation.
Evidence still needed
Predefine an empty-community treatment or obtain balanced biological observations that separate ecological structure from library effort.

02 / IMPERFECT DETECTION

Withheld

Initial claim

A correction model can recover the true occupancy of rare Conwy labels.

→

Credibility audit

A 648-scenario, 100-simulation known-truth atlas compared naive occurrence, replication-aware and depth-aware estimators under depth coupling, missingness and mask error.

→

Surviving wording

Known-truth simulations identify design regions where fast occupancy estimators can or cannot recover their target; they do not repair or validate the unconverged real Conwy correction.

Inspect gate, limitation and evidence
Gate result
Synthetic atlas: 613/1,944 estimator-scenarios recoverable, 732 unreliable and 599 non-identifiable. The real Conwy recovery fit still fails its convergence/mask gate.
Limitation
The atlas is synthetic and conditional on its generator. It cannot estimate Conwy occupancy, fill unsurveyed cells, or convert a failed real fit into biological evidence.
Evidence still needed
Collect a design with independent biological truth, balanced replication and depth separated from the river gradient, then validate recovery before interpreting rare-label coefficients.

03 / PIPELINE DISAGREEMENT

Survives

Initial claim

Source-positive cells are more likely to be absent from the reprocessed table when source signal or the reprocessed raw-name-table count total is low.

→

Credibility audit

The analysis used exactly 2,344 source-positive cells, paired 999-draw site/time/taxon block bootstraps and 43 leave-outs. Because the preregistered 35-column total includes Unassigned and the focal count, a focal-excluded 34-column assigned-fish sensitivity removed that direct part-whole coupling.

→

Surviving wording

Within the verified shared-library comparison, lower source-study relative abundance and a lower reprocessed assigned-fish count total excluding the focal label are associated with pipeline non-recovery.

Inspect gate, limitation and evidence
Gate result
Source signal passed in the preregistered model: coefficient -1.574 (bootstrap 95% interval -2.950 to -0.807). The focal-excluded assigned-fish total also passed: -1.149 (-3.086 to -0.579), 999/999 finite fits and 43/43 leave-outs negative.
Limitation
Neither count total is total library sequencing depth, and both are processed, potentially endogenous quantities. The outcome is disagreement relative to the source table. Neither pipeline is biological ground truth, so this is not a causal effect, abundance effect or biological false-negative rate.
Evidence still needed
Use mock communities, technical replicates and independently quantified templates to partition extraction, sequencing, filtering and assignment losses.

04 / TAXONOMIC RESOLUTION

Withheld

Initial claim

Longer mitochondrial markers consistently resolve the relevant public Salmo species labels more finely than the MiFish fragment.

→

Credibility audit

Accession-backed MiFish 12S, COI and cyt-b panels measured exact sharing, barcode gaps and all-ties leave-one-accession-out classification, with two outgroup genera and explicit coverage gates.

→

Surviving wording

The longer-marker species comparison is withheld. A separate, narrower record-level result is that MiFish 12S, COI and cyt-b each passed the audited Salmo-versus-outgroup genus boundary.

Inspect gate, limitation and evidence
Gate result
Species comparison was coverage-inadequate for every marker: 2 of 7 candidate Salmo labels were eligible; the preregistered minimum was 4. In that narrowed two-label diagnostic, all 10/10 queries per marker retained their submitted label; the separate genus boundary passed 10/10 against each outgroup for all three markers.
Limitation
Seven of 21 selected records, including all five S. salar records, lack voucher/isolate/strain source keys, so their specimen independence is unverified. Submitted database labels are annotations, not truth; the benchmark cannot establish ASV0001 species identity, local occurrence, stock, ancestry, hybrid status or abundance.
Evidence still needed
Add independently curated, voucher-backed records for at least four relevant Salmo labels and re-run the frozen all-ties species gate.

05 / TRAIT DIVERSITY

Failed Gate

Initial claim

Downstream functional-trait diversity broadens more than expected from detected-label richness and prevalence alone.

→

Credibility audit

Rao's Q used a documented equal-axis Gower matrix and 9,999 fixed-margin checkerboard null draws, preserving unit richness and label prevalence within each timepoint; a strict five-label sensitivity repeated the test.

→

Surviving wording

Both observed downstream trait-diversity contrasts were slightly above their null means, but neither was unusually large under the fixed-margin null; the proposed broadening beyond richness and prevalence is unsupported.

Inspect gate, limitation and evidence
Gate result
Failed in both panels: primary one-sided p = 0.3159; strict sensitivity p = 0.1142.
Limitation
The result is conditional on analysis-label assignments and cited trait proxies. It says nothing about abundance, diet, realised habitat use, causal filtering or independently verified occurrence.
Evidence still needed
Use verified species occurrences, population-specific traits and independent abundance or habitat measurements before proposing a causal functional mechanism.
